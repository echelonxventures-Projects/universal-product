# Money — the canonical model (PR-016)

The written contract for how the platform represents, computes, stores and exchanges money.
The decisions this model leaves to the owner are recorded, with their options, in
[docs/money-decisions.md](money-decisions.md); none is approved.
Code: `lib/money.js` (the contract), `lib/money-store.js` (money at rest), `lib/refunds.js`
(refunds), configuration: `lib/seed/money.json`. Inventory: `docs/money-inventory.generated.md`
(generated by `npm run money:inventory`). Release status: `PRODUCTION_READINESS.md` PR-016.

## 1. Scope

**The payment spine** — every amount a shopper is charged, a store is paid, or money is returned:

| Table | Amounts | Currency on the row |
|---|---|---|
| `orders` | `sub` (goods after offers), `ship_fee`, `gift_used`, `tax`, `total` | `currency` (the shown currency the order was charged in) |
| `order_items` | `price` (unit price shown) | `currency` (added; from its order) |
| `payments` | `amount`, `fee`, and `refunded_minor` | `currency` |
| `refunds` (new) | `amount_minor` | `currency` |
| `invoices`, `invoice_lines`, `credit_notes` | `amount`; `unit_amount`, `amount`, `tax`; `amount` | `currency` (`invoice_lines.currency` added) |
| `settlements` | `gross`, `commission`, `net` | `currency` (added — it never recorded one) |
| `disputes` | `amount` | `currency` |
| `payouts`, `payout_items` | `gross`, `fee`, `net`; `amount` | `currency` (now written — a payout used to be credited in no currency) |

The Universal Wallet (`lib/wallet.js`) already keeps exact minor units (`ledger_postings.amount
INTEGER`); `wallets.balance` and `wallet_entries.*` are projections written only from it.

**Not in this scope** (classified, still `REAL`, listed in the inventory and in
`PRODUCTION_READINESS.md` PR-045): the money columns of the other domains — fintech (loans,
BNPL, bills, recharges), mobility fares, payroll, affiliates, ads, auctions, peer listings,
operations budgets, GOIP settlements. Their amounts never reach a payment provider through the
checkout or invoice path.

## 2. The contract (`lib/money.js`)

| Kind | Representation | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| **Money** | `{ minor: BigInt, currency: "KWD", scale: 3 }`, frozen | `of(amount, cur)` (must already be exact at the scale — more decimals are refused), `rounded(amount, cur)` (rounded once by the currency's rule), `ofMinor(minor, cur, scale)` (as stored) |
| **Rate** (exchange rate, tax rate, factor) | a decimal, read exactly; never Money | — |
| **Percentage** | a rate × 100 (commission 5 = 5 %) | — |
| **Quantity** | a count or measure; never Money | — |

- **Precision is data.** A currency's scale is its `decimals` in Value's currency master
  (`currencies`). A currency the master does not know is refused (`currency_unknown`); an amount
  with no currency is refused (`currency_missing`). No currency, country or scale is assumed —
  no "2 decimals unless listed", no default currency inferred from a place.
- **One currency, one scale.** `add`, `sub`, `sum`, `cmp` refuse two currencies
  (`currency_mismatch`) or one currency at two scales (`scale_mismatch`). The only way an amount
  changes currency is `convertTo(m, rate, toCurrency)`.
- **Sign and range.** Amounts may be negative (credits, discounts). Arithmetic is BigInt and
  unbounded; a stored or projected amount must be a safe integer of minor units
  (|minor| ≤ 2⁵³ − 1 ≈ 9.0 × 10¹⁵, i.e. 90 trillion at two decimals); anything larger is
  refused (`money_range`), never silently lost.
- **Numbers coming in.** A JS number is read through its decimal spelling: exact for any value
  written with up to 15 significant digits (DBL_DIG). A spelling longer than that is the residue
  of binary arithmetic upstream (`0.1 + 0.2` is `0.30000000000000004`) and is read at 15
  significant digits. Amounts that genuinely need more digits are passed as strings or BigInt.
- **Malformed input** — `NaN`, `Infinity`, `"12,34"`, a fractional scale, an unknown rule — is
  refused.

| Operation | Function | Currency / scale | Rounds? |
|---|---|---|---|
| parse, exact | `of` | the currency's | never (refuses excess digits) |
| parse, rounded | `rounded` | the currency's | once, by the currency's rule |
| add, subtract, negate, sum | `add`, `sub`, `neg`, `sum` | must match | never |
| × whole quantity | `mulQty(m, 3)` | m's | never |
| × fractional quantity / rate | `mulQty(m, "2.5")`, `mulRate(m, "0.18")` | m's | once |
| × percentage | `pct(m, 5)` | m's | once |
| ÷ factor | `divRate(m, "1.18")` | m's | once |
| split / allocate | `split(m, n)`, `allocateMoney(m, weights)` | m's | never — parts sum exactly (largest remainder, ties to the earlier part) |
| convert | `convertTo(m, rate, cur)` | target's | once, by the target's rule |
| compare | `cmp`, `eq`, `min`, `max`, `isZero` | must match | — |
| format | `toString` → `"12.340"` | m's | — |
| projection | `toNumber` — the double whose shortest spelling is the exact decimal; for display and legacy readers, never for computing | | — |

**Serialization v1** (`toJSON` / `fromJSON`):
`{ "v": 1, "amount": "12.340", "currency": "KWD", "minor": "12340", "scale": 3, "rounding": "half-up" }`
— strings for the amount and the minor units, so no reader turns them into a float. A Money
value cannot be passed to `JSON.stringify` by accident: its BigInt throws. `fromJSON` refuses an
unknown version and an amount whose `minor` and `amount` disagree. Checkout, the cart, the
checkout quote, the order response, `order.created`/`order.paid` events, refunds, invoices and
credit notes carry this form beside the legacy number fields.

### Currency roles

| Role | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Base** | the store's pricing currency (its country's currency) | catalogue prices, offers, wallet spend at checkout, tax computation |
| **Shown / transaction** | the currency the shopper sees and is charged in (the base unless the platform allows a store override, `currencyOverridesAllowed`) | the order row, the payment row, the gateway request, the receipt |
| **Settlement** | the currency a provider pays out in | not modelled — the provider's own record is authoritative (gate 2/3, PR-025) |
| **Wallet unit** | a wallet's own unit | checkout spends only wallets in the base currency |
| **Platform billing** | the platform's own currency | plans, module prices, invoices to stores |

Base → shown is the only conversion on the checkout path: one rate per order (`orders.fx`),
each component converted once. Indicative storefront conversion (the `storefront.currency_switch`
gate) is display only and is not shown on the checkout page.

## 3. The rounding policy

**The rule.** One named rule per currency, from `lib/seed/money.json`: a platform default
(`rounding`) and per-currency overrides (`currencies.XXX.rounding`). Rules: `half-up` (half away
from zero), `half-even`, `half-down`, `up`, `down`, `ceiling`, `floor`. **The configured default
is `half-up`, an engineering default awaiting the owner's written policy** (gate 4) — it is the
common commercial rule and matches what receipts are expected to show; `half-even` removes the
upward bias of half-up over many roundings and is the usual choice where an authority requires
it. Changing the rule is a configuration change, applied everywhere at once.

**Where rounding happens** — once, at a document boundary, never in between:

| Point | Rule |
|---|---|
| A bag line | unit price × quantity, rounded once in the base currency |
| An offer's discount | rounded once; never more than the goods |
| Shipping, a surcharge, each fee or tip line | each rounded once |
| Tax | the exact sum of (amount × rate) — per line when `checkout.tax_per_line`, else on the document — rounded **once for the document**, at the currency's scale |
| Inclusive tax | net = amount ÷ (1 + rate), rounded once; tax = amount − net |
| Base → shown | each component converted once; the **shown total is the exact sum of the shown components** (the receipt adds up; that is what is charged) |
| A bag line as shown | the shown goods allocated across the lines in proportion to their exact base amounts, so the lines add up to the goods |
| A gateway fee estimate | percentage of the charge plus the fixed fee, rounded once |
| An invoice line | unit × quantity, rounded once; proration ÷ 30 once; usage in another currency is not billed on the invoice |
| A settlement | Σ unit × quantity of its lines (exact), per seller **per currency**; commission rounded once |
| A refund | exact, in the payment's currency at its scale; never rounded |

## 4. Money at rest (`lib/money-store.js`)

- Each spine amount column gains `<column>_minor INTEGER` (SQLite 64-bit; Postgres `BIGINT`),
  and each row `money_scale` (its currency's decimals when written), `currency` (where it had
  none) and `money_state` (`NULL`, or `quarantined`). **The minor units are the financial
  truth.** `payments.refunded_minor` sums the payment's reserved refunds.
- The `REAL` column beside each is a **projection**: the double whose shortest spelling is the
  exact decimal, written only from the minor value (`MS.insert`, `MS.update`).
- **The database refuses drift.** SQLite: `BEFORE INSERT`/`UPDATE` triggers `money1_<table>_*`;
  Postgres: `CHECK` constraints `money1_<table>_projection` added by `lib/migrate.js`, from the
  same expression: for every amount, both null, or the minor set and
  `REAL = CAST(minor AS REAL) / 10^scale` (a correctly rounded division gives the same double as
  parsing the decimal), and `0 ≤ refunded_minor ≤ amount_minor`. A row in quarantine is exempt.
  A writer that still writes only the `REAL` column is refused — it cannot silently create an
  amount with no exact value.
- **Readers of money decisions read minor units** (`MS.amountOf`): refunds and their limits,
  provider amount checks, reconciliation, invoice collection, credit-note limits, settlements,
  payouts, claims, the shopper's tax-invoice document. A quarantined row is refused there
  (`money_review`).

### The transitional projection — end date and removal

The `REAL` projections are a compatibility layer for readers that predate this (reports,
analytics, consoles, exports). They are not a second source of truth: they are written only from
the minor value, and the database guard makes a divergent value impossible to write.
**Verification:** `npm run money:verify` (and the migration's own self-check) — zero mismatched,
zero unconverted. **Removal criteria:** every reader listed by `node scripts/money-audit.js`
reads minor units or the v1 serialization; then migration `money-0002` drops the projections.
**End date:** to be set by the owner with the release plan; proposed: within two releases of
launch.

## 5. Migration `money-0001`

Runs at boot, **before the server listens**, in **one transaction**, once (recorded in
`money_migrations`). Fresh installs write exact amounts from the start; the migration then finds
nothing to convert. On an existing database every spine row is read in key order, a batch at a
time, and each amount classified:

| Class | Rule | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **exact** | the REAL's shortest spelling has no more decimals than the currency | minor = that decimal |
| **normalized** | the REAL is binary residue around exactly one such decimal (at 15 significant digits: `0.30000000000000004` → `0.3`) | minor = that decimal; projection rewritten; source value recorded in `money_quarantine` with status `normalized` for audit |
| **quarantined** | genuinely more decimals than the currency (`10.235` USD), no currency, a currency the master does not know, or not a number | the row keeps every source value, `money_state = quarantined`, an `open` entry in `money_quarantine` with the candidates (`half-up`, `half-even`, `down`) and the configured rule. **Nothing is guessed.** |

Then the guard is installed and the migration checks its own result (every row satisfies the
guard) before committing; any failure rolls everything back and the boot fails — a database that
cannot be migrated serves no traffic.

- **Interrupted** (crash, kill, power): the transaction rolls back; the added columns are empty;
  the next boot runs it again and reaches the same result (`test/money-store.js`).
- **Rerun**: a finished migration is never re-run.
- **Resolution**: `node scripts/money.js quarantine` lists open entries; an operator decides —
  `node scripts/money.js resolve <id> <minor> --by <name> [--currency XXX] --note …`, or
  `POST /api/platform/money/quarantine/:id/resolve` — recorded with who, when and why, audited;
  the source value stays in the quarantine record; the row is released when its last open entry
  is resolved.
- **Postgres**: receives the migrated schema, data and constraints through `lib/migrate.js`,
  which refuses a SQLite source whose money migration has not finished. A Postgres database
  without it is refused at boot. (Upgrading an existing Postgres database in place is not
  supported — PR-007.)
- **Rollback**: take a backup before upgrading (`npm run backup`, DEPLOYMENT_RUNBOOK.md). The
  change is additive (no column dropped or rewritten except normalized projections), but code
  from before it writes `REAL` only and is refused by the guard; rolling back the code therefore
  means restoring the pre-upgrade backup (DATABASE_RECOVERY_RUNBOOK.md). Orders taken after the
  upgrade would then need re-entry — prefer a forward fix.

Measured (Apple silicon, SQLite, one process, synthetic data; reproduce with
`node scripts/money-perf.js 200000`): 200,001 orders + 400,002 lines + 200,000 payments +
50,002 invoices converted in 21.4 s on Node 22.21.1 and on Node 26.11, peak RSS 392 MB
including `verify()`; the next boot 2.6 s.

## 6. Refunds (`lib/refunds.js`)

- A refund is a row: an exact amount of the payment's currency, its source (console, manual,
  provider), its idempotency key, the provider's refund id, attempts, and its state in the
  `refund` machine (`lib/seed/states.json`): `pending`, `unknown` (reserved, open),
  `succeeded` (reserved, success), `failed` (nothing reserved).
- **Never more than was captured**: one conditional `UPDATE` reserves the amount
  (`amount_minor − refunded_minor ≥ amount`); two processes racing for the last of a payment —
  one succeeds, one is refused (`test/money-store.js`). The database guard repeats the bound.
- **Once**: the same Idempotency-Key and amount returns the first outcome; a different amount
  under the same key is refused (422). A partial refund requires a key; a full refund may come
  without one (it can only happen once).
- **Provider outcomes**: a refusal releases the reservation; **no answer** (throw, timeout) keeps
  it as `unknown` — neither reported as refunded nor released — until staff resolve it from the
  provider's record (`POST /api/admin/refunds/:id/resolve`); a provider answering with a refund
  id already recorded is not counted as a new refund (`unknown`).
- **Partial at the provider**: a live partial refund goes through the provider only if its
  refund connector sends the amount; otherwise it is refused (`partial_unsupported`) and must be
  done at the provider and recorded with `manual: true`. Provider events are applied by their
  declared meaning — `cumulative` (the total refunded so far) or `refund` (one refund, by id).
- **Effects**: when the reserved refunds reach the whole amount and all have succeeded, the
  payment moves to its `reversed` state and the full-refund effects run (offer redemptions
  reversed, `order.refunded`). **A partial refund apportions no tax and reverses no offer** —
  that is the owner's decision (PR-029).

## 7. Operations

- `npm run money:verify` — every spine amount's projection equals its minor units, nothing is
  unconverted, every payment's refunded total equals its reserved refunds; exits 1 otherwise.
  Run it after a restore and before and after an upgrade.
- `GET /api/platform/money` — the same, with the open quarantine.
- `GET /api/admin/payments/:id/refunds` — a payment's refunds and what is left to refund.
- `npm run money:inventory` — regenerates the inventory; `node scripts/money-audit.js --check`
  (in `npm test`) fails on an unclassified `REAL` column or money rounded outside `lib/money.js`
  in a spine module.
