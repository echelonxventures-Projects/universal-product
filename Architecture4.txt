# UCOS Ω∞ — UNIVERSAL INDUSTRY · PRODUCT · SERVICE · COMMERCE UNIVERSE

Knowledge Once · Reuse Everywhere · Zero Hard Coding · Infinite Extensibility

This document is the canonical declaration of the universal industry, product, service
and commerce universe. It is parsed, not coded: every universe and registry list below
is registered into the platform's knowledge graph by the parser, instantiated as a live
data-defined entity, governed through the foundation lifecycle, and activated into the
catalog — with zero per-universe code (Architecture3 Article 4.2).
