
# UCOS Ω∞

# UNIVERSAL CIVILIZATION OPERATING SYSTEM (UCOS Ω∞)

Version: Constitutional Foundation v1.0

---

# PREAMBLE

UCOS Ω∞ is a universal, metadata-driven, ontology-first, registry-driven,
industry-agnostic platform capable of modeling, governing, composing,
executing and evolving any product, service, capability, process,
organization, industry, marketplace, ecosystem, intelligence system or
civilization-scale construct.

The objective is not to build an e-commerce platform, ERP, CRM,
marketplace, booking engine, offer engine, CMS, workflow engine or AI
platform. The objective is to discover and construct the universal
primitives from which all such systems can be composed.

This constitution governs itself. Every artifact it produces is derived
from primitives it defines, and no primitive is introduced where an
existing one suffices.
