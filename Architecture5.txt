# UCOS Ω∞ — UNIVERSAL CAPABILITY PLATFORM · MASTER ARCHITECTURE REFERENCE

Knowledge Once · Zero Hard Coding · Zero Duplication · Zero Overlap · Infinite Composability

This document is the canonical declaration of the universal capability platform itself: the
constitutional foundation, the commerce knowledge universe, the business capability surface, the
intelligence fabric, the governance and trust universes, the evolution and platform factory
universes, and the future existence layer. It is parsed, not coded: every universe and registry
list below is registered into the platform's knowledge graph by the parser, instantiated as a
live data-defined entity, governed through the foundation lifecycle, and activated into the
catalog — with zero per-universe code (Architecture3 Article 4.2).

The declaration is deliberately disjoint from the Industry · Product · Service · Commerce Universe
(Architecture4): that corpus owns the commerce surface, this one owns the platform's constitutional,
capability, intelligence, governance, evolution and factory layers. No universe is declared twice.
