# LXM canonical decision register. build.py renders Part C of the Master from it; assimilate.py and validate.py read it.
# Head line: @LXM-DEC-NNNNNN | status | group. Status: adopted (binding where its outcome applies) or open (decides nothing).
# Group: the Part C table that shows it. Fields, one per line as "key: value":
#   label short name; q question or conflict; o outcome; w adopted when and by whom; p approval provenance; r rationale;
#   f facts; opt options and consequences; rec recommendation (not approval);
#   e normative effect in this master; a mutation-authorization scope: source clauses (SRC-NNNN §n) and records, or None (empty).
# Status, effect and scope are separate: an empty scope authorizes no change and does not make a decision inactive.
# Only the owner adopts a decision. An adopted decision is never edited, only superseded by a later one.
# Table layouts: "%group | Header=key | ...". The ID column shows the ID, then " (label)" if there is one.

%C.3 | ID=id | Decision=o | Adopted=w | Approval provenance=p | Rationale=r | Effect in this master=e | Authorizes=a
%C.2 | ID=id | Decision=q | Facts=f | Options and consequences=opt | Recommendation (not approval)=rec
