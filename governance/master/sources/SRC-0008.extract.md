# LUXORA Ω∞ — MASTER ASSIMILATION-FIRST CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLE

**Principle ID:** LXM-ACP-001  
**Name:** Continuous Canonical Knowledge Assimilation  
**Authority:** Foundational Architectural Principle

## 1. Fundamental Law

LUXORA shall operate through continuous, validation-driven assimilation into its canonical Master.

The Master shall not depend on periodic manual audits, repeated document reconciliation, duplicate removal exercises or deferred completeness reviews to maintain its integrity.

Every new contribution, modification, extension, decision, requirement, capability, specification, configuration, implementation artifact or evidence item must pass through the Master Assimilation Engine before becoming an accepted canonical contribution.

## 2. Knowledge Assimilation

The system shall discover and evaluate the contribution against the entire relevant canonical knowledge environment.

It shall detect and resolve:

- Exact and semantic duplication.
- Full and partial overlap.
- Complementary and composable knowledge.
- Missing relationships and dependencies.
- Contradictions and competing authorities.
- Superseded and obsolete knowledge.
- Incomplete, ambiguous and unsupported assertions.
- Existing canonical objects that should be extended rather than duplicated.
- Genuinely new concepts that require canonical identity.

## 3. Canonical Mutation

Each accepted contribution shall result in the minimum correct canonical mutation.

Permitted outcomes include recognition, extension, composition, refinement, governed replacement, supersession or creation of a new canonical object.

The system shall preserve source provenance, authority, semantic meaning, version history, dependencies and traceability.

No accepted contribution may create an unnecessary parallel authority or duplicate source of truth.

## 4. Validation Before Acceptance

Applicable conformance requirements must be evaluated before canonical publication.

Validation shall cover identity, semantic consistency, completeness, authority, uniqueness, scope, dependencies, relationships, governance, compatibility, security, lifecycle and evidence.

A failed validation shall prevent acceptance and create a traceable exception with a reason, impact and required resolution.

No invalid contribution may be silently accepted.

## 5. Automatic Propagation

Following successful assimilation, dependent indexes, registries, relationships, specifications, documentation, conformance views, test obligations and implementation traceability shall be updated or regenerated from canonical knowledge.

Derived representations shall never become competing authorities.

## 6. Continuous Integrity

Every canonical mutation shall preserve the applicable Master invariants.

Integrity shall be enforced at ingestion and change time through automated validation, transaction controls, invariant checks and continuous verification.

A later audit shall not be the primary mechanism for discovering preventable omissions, duplication, overlap, inconsistency or broken traceability.

## 7. Human Governance

Human intervention shall be required only where authority, policy, unresolved contradiction, material ambiguity, reserved ownership or consequential risk genuinely requires a human decision.

Routine classification, matching, propagation, indexing, consistency checking and traceability maintenance shall be automated to the maximum extent supported by reliable evidence.

## 8. Acceptance Criterion

A new contribution is not considered complete merely because it has been received, documented, implemented or tested.

It is complete only when it has been correctly assimilated into the canonical Master, all applicable invariants pass, dependencies and derived representations are consistent, and its lineage and conformance evidence are recorded.

**The Master must continuously become more complete, coherent and internally consistent as new knowledge enters it.**

This is a preventive architectural invariant, not a periodic cleanup objective.
