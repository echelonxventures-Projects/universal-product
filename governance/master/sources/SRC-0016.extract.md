### 4. Implement open-ended technology registration

Implement a canonical, extensible technology registry that does not enumerate all permitted technology categories in application code.

The model must support registration and discovery of existing and future categories, tools and technologies through governed definitions and extension contracts.

For each applicable registered item, support metadata such as:

- Identity, category, supplier, origin and provenance.
- Declared capabilities and requirements.
- Dependencies and compatibility constraints.
- Available releases, artifacts and release channels.
- Security advisories, integrity and provenance evidence.
- Licensing and operational constraints.
- Supported environments and execution characteristics.
- Verification results and test evidence.
- Lifecycle state, authorization and permitted usage.
- Replacement, deprecation, rollback and migration relationships.

These are extensible metadata contracts, not a finite list of technology classes. New categories must be registrable without modifying the governance core merely to add another category name.

A new discovery protocol or computational behavior may require a new adapter. The adapter must integrate with the same canonical registry and governance lifecycle.

### 5. Implement discovery, evaluation and resolution

Separate discovery from approval and actual use.

Implement a governed lifecycle capable of distinguishing:

- Discovered.
- Candidate.
- Under evaluation.
- Compatible.
- Verified.
- Approved.
- Resolved for a specific use.
- Active.
- Deprecated.
- Rejected or prohibited.

Discover releases and metadata from appropriate authoritative sources through extensible discovery adapters. Validate their provenance and metadata. Evaluate compatibility, security, dependencies and required tests before approving use.

Do not declare a runtime, language, library, tool or future technology compatible merely because its metadata exists.

Do not implement a blind `latest` selector for production. Select a verified, reproducible resolution and record its artifact identity, dependency information, provenance and test evidence.
