2. Quick Start Guide (5 Minutes)

Follow these steps to get started:

1 Login to Platform Console

Click the Open Platform Console button above, or visit:

http://localhost:8787/platform.html

Sign in with:

Email: platform@luxora.shop | Password: platform123

2 Configure Global Settings

View Global Configuration Steps

3 Launch Your First Store

In Platform Console:

  1. Click 🏬 Stores in the left menu
  2. Click Launch Wizard
  3. Fill in store name, choose jurisdiction (country), select plan

Store created! You'll receive admin credentials.

4 Access Store Console

In Platform Console → 🏬 Stores → Click on your store name

Opens: http://localhost:8787/admin.html

5 Add Products

In Store Console:

  1. Click 📦 Products in the left menu
  2. Click New Product
  3. Fill in title, brand, department, price, stock
✅ You're ready! Your platform is configured and your first store is set up.
