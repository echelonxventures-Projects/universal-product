## 1. What BC-5 recorded

`BC-5-DECISION-MEMOS.md` records D-1 (INVARIANT) and D-2 (Option A), and each decision's impact
row states *"Authority required: Constitutional / amendment — **NOT LOCATED**."* That qualification

## 2. The location, derived from two instruments

The office is defined in one place and its holder in another. Neither alone closes the question;
together they do.

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| What is Amendment Authority? | The authority to admit an amendment, authorize successor creation, and record evolution. It confers no acceptance, certification, ratification or freeze itself | `CEP-009` Article I.1 |
| Who holds it? | **Tier 1 is occupied by the ratified UCOS Ω∞ Constitution of substance itself** — not a promotion of any lower instrument | `UCOS-RAT-002` D-RAT-02-02 |
| Where is that holder registered? | `02-MASTER/UCOS-ABSOLUTE-CONSTITUTIONAL-LAWS-REGISTER.md` is its registered in-corpus definitional home | `UCOS-RAT-002` D-RAT-02-02 |
| Was the vacancy closed? | Yes — `VAC-01` closed at `UCOS-RAT-002`, and `00-CMG/CMG-REGISTRY.json:2549` records it | `CMG-REGISTRY.json` |

**Amendment Authority is therefore LOCATED.** The holder is the ratified constitution of
substance; its registered home is the laws register. The office is not a person and was never
going to be — CEP-009 I.2 binds it to evolve "only through successor creation," and I.5 forbids
it being "self-conferred by Execution Authority."

## 3. What this does and does not mean

- **It means** the BC-5 decisions D-1/D-2 are recorded under a *located* authority rather than
  an unlocated one, and the "NOT LOCATED" qualification is withdrawn.
- **It does not mean** the decisions are *ratified*. Locating an office is not the act of that
  office. The constitution of substance holds the amendment authority; whether it confirms or
  supersedes D-1/D-2 is determined by reading the constitution, which is what §4 does.
- **It does not mean** a human's authorization is evidenced. The owner's direction to record
  these decisions remains the evidence on record; no engine act substitutes for it.

## 5. Boundary

This record locates an office and measures two decisions against it. It does not amend a
constitution, does not ratify a decision, and does not exercise the amendment authority —
CEP-009 I.4 forbids it from governing, executing, validating, certifying, ratifying, freezing or
creating evidence. If the owner judges that D-1 or D-2 is wrong, that act supersedes this record;
it does not erase it.
