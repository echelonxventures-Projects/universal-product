## Decision: NO-GO for production today

Use the repository-side work as the basis for a **staging rehearsal**, not a production launch. The
code no longer has a known critical defect that makes a production deployment unsafe by itself.
Launch is blocked by things this repository cannot supply:

1. **No live payment provider has been verified** (PR-025). In production, card checkout is now
   declined until a live gateway is connected (PR-001), so the store can only take cash on
   delivery and bank transfer until one is.
2. **No staging environment, deployment target, DNS/TLS or secret store** has been named
   (PR-026, PR-027).
3. **Provider-side events are applied, but never against a live provider** (PR-014). Signed
   webhooks and a lookup sweep move pending payments to captured or failed; refunds (full and
   partial) and chargebacks made at the provider are applied, each event once (PR-021). Nothing
   is verified against a provider's sandbox.
4. **The owner has not written the rounding policy or approved the money scope** (PR-016,
   gate 4). The payment spine is now exact end to end — integer minor units at rest with a
   database guard, one money contract, a quarantining migration — but the configured rule
   (`half-up`) is an engineering default, and the money of the other domains (fintech, mobility,
   payroll, affiliates, ads…) is still `REAL` (PR-045).
5. **Partial refunds are modelled and enforced, but not live-verified, and their tax and offer
   effects are not decided** (PR-029). A refund can never exceed what was captured and is
   applied once; a partial refund apportions no tax and reverses no offer until the owner
   decides how.
6. **Off-host backups, alerting, on-call, staging and deployment approval** have to be provided
   by the target environment and the owner (PR-012, PR-015, PR-026, PR-027).

## Money representation (PR-016)

The written contract, rounding policy, migration and rollback are in [docs/money.md](docs/money.md);
the full inventory is [docs/money-inventory.generated.md](docs/money-inventory.generated.md)
(regenerated by `npm run money:inventory`).

**Inventory (recomputed on `c9e63f4`, then after this pass).** 242 `REAL` columns, every one
classified by meaning in `scripts/money-inventory.json`: money amounts, unit prices / monetary
rates, exchange rates, quantities, ratios and measurements — quantities, ratios and measurements
(coordinates, ratings, distances, weights, hours, tax and commission rates) are not money and
are not migrated. The **payment spine** — 21 amount columns of `orders`, `order_items`, `payments`,
`invoices`, `invoice_lines`, `credit_notes`, `settlements`, `disputes`, `payouts`,
`payout_items`, plus the new `refunds` — is converted; the wallet ledger was already exact; the
other domains' money is PR-045. Code: 132 `toFixed(2)` sites in `lib/` before; a tokenizer
(strings, comments, regexes and template text removed, so a match is code) now finds 0 money
roundings outside `lib/money.js` in the spine modules, and 139 money-classed sites in other
domains.

**Decision.** Integer minor units (`<column>_minor`, SQLite INTEGER / Postgres BIGINT) at the
row's `money_scale` in the row's `currency`, computed through one BigInt contract. Not SQLite
`NUMERIC` (its affinity stores most decimals as binary doubles) and not decimal text (no exact
comparison or summation in SQL). Postgres `NUMERIC` would be exact but is not available on
SQLite, the release database. The `REAL` columns remain as projections, enforced equal by the
database, until their readers move (docs/money.md §4).

**Migration.** `money-0001`, at boot, before listening, one self-checking transaction; exact /
normalized (audited) / quarantined (source kept, candidates listed, operator decision recorded).
**No production or staging database exists to migrate** (PR-026), so how many real records a
live install would quarantine is unknown until it is booted on this code; the operator then
runs `node scripts/money.js quarantine` and resolves each before refunds or settlements touch
those rows.
